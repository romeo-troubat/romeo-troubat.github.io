Research Statement

I am studying discrete subgroups of SO(p,q) and geometric structures associated to them. I am particularily interested in the generalization of theorems of Lorentzian geometry to the higher signature case and in the study of Cartan geometries modeled on the flag manifolds of SO(p,q).
